# Official Assets

This directory contains official product assets for AI Visual Composer.

These assets are intentionally versioned because they are part of the current product behavior. They are not local runtime dependencies, generated outputs, model files, checkpoints, or private development resources.

## Asset Governance

- Official product assets may be committed when they are required by the product.
- Asset size alone is not a reason to remove an official asset while repository size remains reasonable for the project.
- Future overlays may be committed when they become official product assets.
- Development-only assets, local experiments, generated media, AI models, checkpoints, LoRAs, VAE files, embeddings, ControlNet resources, external runtimes, logs, and local outputs must remain outside the public repository.
- New assets must have confirmed provenance and redistribution permission before being committed.
- Asset-specific license notices may be added in the future when a specific asset requires one.
- Every new official asset added to the project must document its provenance, license, or conditions of use when it is added to the repository.

Unless an asset-specific notice states otherwise, versioned assets in this directory are distributed as official repository assets under the project license.

## Music Asset Rights

The music resources included in `assets/music/` are official assets of AI Visual Composer.

They were generated using the owner's Suno account and are covered by the license associated with that account.

Their inclusion in this repository does not transfer rights to third parties. Any use, redistribution, or reuse of these music resources must respect the conditions of the license associated with those resources and the rights of the owner.

This policy exists to keep the official asset inventory complete, traceable, and professionally documented.
